**First up, we have [Walking Simulator v1.1](https://codetorch.net/projects/230020)!** 

The ID is "230020" which, is aesthetically pleasing, so props for that! Its simple, and not hard to figure out, so points for that! It has nice scenery, but my only problem is the animation feels a little bit choppy. 

**Final rating: 8.6/10**



**Next we have [3D Sonic Online!](https://codetorch.net/projects/256330)**

It's fun, great sprites, and nice 3d, not much gameplay from what I've seen, but that's mainly due to the fact I was the only one online! Camera movement is a little slow, but the game itself is pretty good!

**Final rating: 7.9/10**
