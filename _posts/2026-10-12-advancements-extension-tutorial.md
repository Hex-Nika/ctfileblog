Welcome to another Codetorch extensions tutorial! Today, we will be learning how to properly use the advancements extension!

#### Tips:
Make sure to only give the advancement if they don't have it, otherwise it could get annoying with the constant popups

Use a relevant image, sometimes people just put random stuff as the image, don't use an asset from your project to show what it is.

Make advancements worth something, don't just give out advancements, give them for major milestones, like defeating a hard boss, because remember, you can only have 10 per project!
