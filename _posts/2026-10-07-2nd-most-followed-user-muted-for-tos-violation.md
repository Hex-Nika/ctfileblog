The 2nd most followed user on CodeTorch known as "Lolm8999" or angel, has reportedly been muted for 7 days due to repeated violations of section 3.3 of the CodeTorch TOS. \
On October the 6th 2026 at 10:33 AM EST a report was made to the CodeTorch Discord server, which the contents of would later be posted to CodeTorch by user "Pelmenixx" after being sent the report message by the reporter.

> "yesterday at 8:00 pm the user "lol" AKA angel said they were banned and just 2 hours ago (of this current moment) they had been posting on CodeTorch like it never happened under clause 3.3 of the CodeTorch TOS, this is bannable due to spreading misinformation. "\*\*3.3\*\* You may not use CodeTorch to distribute any content that is false, misleading, or inaccurate." Angel has done this multiple times previously in an attempt to gain a larger following and all the while creating drama on the platform. user "Lol" has 3 alt accounts which they use while they are banned, which is a form of ban evasion. These accounts are: "TRXPPED", "ANGELGONE", and "Lolm8999". Multiple times they have shown that they have gone into DMS with moderators and asked them to ban him, yet posts it, while ban evading on his alt accounts to gain publicity and get more people on his side, convincing them he's being "harassed". I will be gathering more information over the next few days and present it here. Thank you for your time."

This report was then followed up by moderator "MyFaceLeon" at 5:07 PM EST saying he'd given Angel a 7 day mute. The moderator had been yet to make a post, until October 7th at ~6:00pm EST Leon posted 

> "hey guys so, @lol is muted for those of you wondering. he repeatedly breaks code 3.3, and multiple reports of him have come to my attention sooo yer. sorry lol fans, but he needs a week off. ( hes muted for a week )"

We will follow this up as the situation progresses.
